Wheelmate Support

We usually reply within two business days: labs.cluno@gmail.com

  • Registration not found: check the plate, or add your car by hand (make, model, year). Everything else in the app works the same.
  • Lookup unavailable: the DVLA service is sometimes down. Try again later, or add the car by hand.
  • No reminder came: open your phone's Settings, then Notifications, then Wheelmate, and check that notifications are allowed. Then check the date and lead times on the car's page.
  • Imported manual shows no search results: some PDFs are scans of pages with no text, which the app can't search yet. You can still open and read the file.
  • Restore Pro: open Settings and choose Restore purchase. Use the Apple or Google account you bought it with.
  • Moving to a new phone: your data is stored on the phone and isn't backed up by us. An iCloud or iTunes device backup includes it.
  • Delete my data: delete the app. Nothing is kept anywhere else.
  • Privacy Policy: https://bmanga.github.io/car-health-legal/privacy/
  • Terms of Use: https://bmanga.github.io/car-health-legal/terms/

Wheelmate gives general guidance only. If a warning light worries you, stop safely and get it checked.
