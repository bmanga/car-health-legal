Wheelmate Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Wheelmate keeps your car details on your phone. Here is exactly what happens with your data:

  • Your data stays on your device. Your cars, MOT, tax, insurance and service dates, reminders, routine checks, lesson progress and imported owner's manuals are stored in a database on your phone. We never receive them and can't see them.
  • Registration lookup is the only thing that goes online. If you enter a UK registration number to fill in your car's details, the app sends that number to our lookup service. The service passes it to the DVLA and DVSA, and returns your car's make, colour, year, fuel, tax and MOT details to your phone. We don't log or store the registration number or the result.
  • Usage limit. To stop abuse, the lookup service keeps a daily counter. It is tied to a hash of a random code the app creates on your phone, not to your name, your Apple ID or any account. It expires after about a day.
  • Our lookup service runs on Cloudflare. Like any web service, Cloudflare handles your IP address while your request is delivered. We don't keep it.
  • No photos, no AI. Wheelmate doesn't upload photos, doesn't use artificial intelligence, and doesn't use your camera, microphone or location. The "Find my light" picker works on your phone.
  • Owner's manuals. If you import a PDF, it is read and searched on your phone and is never uploaded. "Find official manual" opens the car maker's own website in your browser, which is covered by that site's policy.
  • Reminders are scheduled locally on your device. We don't send push notifications.
  • Purchases. Pro is a one-time purchase. The App Store (or Google Play) handles payment, and we never see your card details. We use RevenueCat to confirm that you own Pro. It receives an anonymous app user ID and your purchase status.
  • No analytics, no ads, no tracking. We don't use analytics or advertising tools, we don't track you across other apps or websites, and we don't sell your information. There are no accounts.
  • Children. Wheelmate is suitable for all ages. We don't knowingly collect personal information from anyone, and no account is needed.
  • Your rights. We hold almost no personal data about you. If you think we hold any, you can ask us for a copy, or to correct or delete it, by emailing us. Under UK law you can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office.
  • Deleting your data. Deleting the app removes everything stored on your device. There is no cloud copy to delete.

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
